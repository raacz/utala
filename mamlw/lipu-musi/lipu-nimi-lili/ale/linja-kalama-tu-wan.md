<div markdown="1" class="sLasina">
“jan sin pi olin mi li lon ala lon?”  
sina toki e ni. taso sina sona ala a e kon  
olin li open lon sewi li kama tan anpa  
jan sin o tawa poka sina sama nanpa  

lon la jan ala li ken sewi sama waso  
sina tawa lon insa pi nasin lili taso  
ni la olin li ken ala awen lon sewi laso  
lon la mi ken kute e kalama unpa tan sewi taso

mi toki e lon ni tawa ale jan  
“olin lon la mi sona ala e tan”  
sona pona li kama tan linja kalama tu wan

</div>
